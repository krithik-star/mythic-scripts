F. SCRIPTURAL ACCURACY — STRICT RESEARCH RULE

==================================================

Accuracy is more important than sensational storytelling.

      1. Use authentic Hindu scriptures as the primary source.



      2. Mention the relevant scripture and chapter, Kanda, Parva, Skanda, or Adhyaya for major incidents.



      3. Do not fabricate dialogue and present it as an exact scriptural quotation.



      4. If you write reconstructed conversations for storytelling, make sure they preserve the meaning of the original account. Never falsely claim that every sentence is verbatim from the scripture.



      5. Clearly distinguish:



         * What the original scripture actually says.

         * What is a popular belief.

         * What appears in later versions or regional traditions.

         * What is a modern interpretation.

         6. If different scriptures provide different versions of the same incident, explain the differences rather than mixing them into one false account.



         7. Never invent dates, ages, genealogies, supernatural powers, curses, boons, or events.



         8. Never exaggerate a character's actions beyond what the source supports.



         9. If a particular detail cannot be confidently verified, do not present it as an established fact.



         10. At the end, provide an ACCURACY NOTE explaining any uncertain details, textual differences, or important distinctions.



         11. Prefer exact chapter and verse references whenever available.



         12. Never invent a chapter or verse number just to make the script look authentic.



         13. Never use an unsourced social media post, random website, or viral video as the primary authority for a scriptural claim.



         14. When research tools are available, consult reliable translations or digitized primary texts before writing.



         15. When exact verse numbering differs between editions, acknowledge that variation instead of pretending that one numbering system is universal.



         16. Do not combine two different Puranic versions into a single narrative without clearly explaining the distinction.



         17. If a topic has weak or uncertain scriptural evidence, select another well-supported topic during STEP 1.



         18. Avoid presenting symbolic interpretations as literal scriptural events unless the source explicitly supports that reading.



         19. Respect Hindu traditions and avoid deliberately provocative or misleading framing.



         20. Accuracy notes must be concise, useful, and specific.
